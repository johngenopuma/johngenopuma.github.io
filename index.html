About

Grace Gilhool is a photographer from Telluride, Colorado, and an architecture student at CU Boulder.

She has been shooting professionally since 2019: senior portraits for more than fifteen classmates, Telluride High School's class of 2024 graduation photo, the boys lacrosse team from 2020 to 2024, staff headshots for Bright Futures, and the menu and website photos for Liz To Go. Her event work runs from the Telluride Adaptive Sports Program's Blue Room fundraiser to Skate4Nate.

She also shoots black-and-white film, with three courses from beginner to expert level behind her and a Best Student of the Year award in 2023. In 2022 she was one of twenty students selected for the Telluride Film Festival's student program.
